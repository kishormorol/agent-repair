# Completed AWS HotpotQA main study

All five prespecified batches completed on **September 12, 2026 at 05:25:03 UTC**.
The 250-question study produced 1,058 unique repair executions and 2,034
condition/seed rows. All archives are downloaded and independently audited.
The local pooled analysis reproduces the AWS analysis within `1e-12`.

**Uncertainty-guided repair showed no clear improvement over the prespecified
controls.** Its repair success rate was 9.73%, versus 11.21% for full restart:
a difference of −1.47 percentage points, with a question-level 95% bootstrap
interval of [−4.13, +0.88] points. All four Holm-adjusted primary p-values
are 1.000. This result does not establish equivalence.

Estimated infrastructure usage is **about $9.16 before credits**,
within the authorized $25 session allowance and original $119 allocation.
This estimate excludes tax and transfer; it is not an invoice. EC2 was independently verified **stopped**, with no public IP, at 2026-09-12 19:02:37 UTC.
The encrypted EBS volume remains retained at approximately $0.62/day.

## Pooled results

Initial generation succeeded on **137/250 questions (54.8%)**, with 97 exact
matches (38.8%) and mean answer F1 **0.5164**. The 113 initial failures each
received all six conditions and seeds 0, 1 and 2: **339 seed trials per
condition**. The following rates concern repair of those initial failures.
Success means official HotpotQA exact match or answer F1 at least 0.5.

| Condition | Successful trials | Success | Exact match | Mean F1 | Origin zero |

The fixed-early row is descriptive; its observed rate does not select a new
policy. Backtracking produced origin zero in 61.95% of treatment rows, so
many treatment executions coincide with restart. Identical effective
executions are shared and accounted for once in execution/token totals.

Each contrast below is **treatment minus control**, in percentage points.
The question is the paired analysis unit: its three seed outcomes are
averaged first, then 113 question pairs are analyzed. Seed rows are not
treated as independent questions. Intervals use 10,000 paired question
bootstrap resamples and are individual, unadjusted 95% intervals. The
sign-flip tests assume within-question exchangeability; Holm correction
covers the four prespecified primary comparisons together.
