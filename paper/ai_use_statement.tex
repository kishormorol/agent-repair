\section*{AI Use Statement}

\paragraph{Language models as the object of study.}
Language models are the subject of this work, not only a tool used to produce
it. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct-AWQ and Mistral-Nemo-Instruct act as the ReAct agents
whose failed trajectories are repaired, and a same-model prompt supplies the
diagnosis/replay comparator. Their versions, revisions, quantisation and
decoding settings are pinned in each frozen protocol and reported in the
appendices.

\paragraph{Generative AI assistance in producing this work.}
An AI coding assistant was used extensively, and we prefer to be specific about
its scope rather than gesture at it. It contributed to: proposing and refining
the hypotheses tested here; the design of the length-matched swap control and
of the exact sign-flip analysis applied to it; the formulation of the
resolution-floor claim that an exact two-sided sign-flip test over $m$ non-zero
blocks cannot report a $p$-value below $2/2^{m}$, together with the
brute-force enumeration used to check it; implementation of the experiment
runners, auditors, statistical code and paper-asset builders; orchestration of
the cloud experiments, including budget gating, monitoring and result
retrieval; reformatting and cleaning of per-trial records for analysis; the
statistical diagnostics reported in the appendices; generation of the figures;
literature lookup and related-work analysis; interpretation of results; and the
drafting and revision of manuscript text, including sections of this
manuscript and the appendix reporting the swap study. The accompanying test
suite was likewise written with AI assistance. No synthetic dataset was
generated: HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA and MuSiQue are used as published, and
the trajectories analysed here are experimental measurements of the agents
under study rather than fabricated data.

\paragraph{What the safeguards do and do not establish.}
The safeguards in this project are structural rather than discretionary. Every
numerical claim in this paper is generated from audited records by a build
step rather than transcribed by hand, and the manuscript fails to build if a
cited quantity has no generating source. Each study is frozen by checksum
before execution and re-analysed independently afterwards, and every reported
cell reproduces with zero mismatches between the on-instance and local
analyses. A supplement reproduces all primary test families from an isolated
extraction. These safeguards constrain arithmetic and provenance tightly. They
establish nothing about whether the design answers the question posed, whether
the estimands are the right ones, or whether a null result is framed fairly.
Much of the design and analysis was produced with AI assistance and then
checked largely by the same process that produced it; self-consistency is not
correctness.

\paragraph{Errors introduced during AI-assisted work.}
Five errors reached execution and were caught before affecting any reported
result: a budget gate wired into two of three call sites, which refused a
controller two minutes into a paid GPU session; an incorrect prediction that
additional matched pairs would lower the resolution floor, when the tie rate
binds instead; an edit that invalidated the checksum provenance of an
already-completed study package; three figures shipped with labels obscured by
legends, found only by inspecting the rendered images; and tests written
against one pandas version that failed on the instance's version, aborting a
run at preflight. Each was found by continued checking rather than by any
process that guarantees detection. We report them because they bear on how
much independent verification a reader should expect of work produced this
way.
